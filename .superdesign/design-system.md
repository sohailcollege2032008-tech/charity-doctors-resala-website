# Atebaa El-Kheir · Resala Nasr City — Design Brief & System

## Product context
Landing website for **أطباء الخير – رسالة – مدينة نصر** ("Charity Doctors", Nasr City branch of Resala Charity Association, Egypt; Resala founded 1999).
A volunteer activity run by medical students and young doctors that delivers free/low-cost healthcare to families in need.
Active since September 2025. Tagline used by the team: **«خير الطب لخير الناس»**. Core belief from their posts: **«الطب رسالة قبل ما يكون مهنة»**.

### Audiences (priority order)
1. Medical students & doctors who may volunteer or join courses/events (main conversion: volunteer sign-up, course registration).
2. Donors & supporters of Resala (conversion: donate via Resala).
3. Families / patients looking for the clinic or next convoy.

### Real content (use this, never invent numbers)
- **Medical convoys (القوافل الطبية)** — named convoys: غيث (Ghaith), VitFam (family vitamins), حساسية الربيع تحت السيطرة (spring allergy), T3 Care, Growing Healthy (breastfeeding/child health), Game of Stones (kidney health), exploratory & awareness convoys. Services: initial exam, blood pressure, blood sugar, history taking, health awareness, charity market.
- **Clinics (العيادات)** — Internal-medicine clinic in المقطم (Mokattam), Fridays and some Mondays; ophthalmology clinic; routine BP & sugar screening. Clinic roles: Head, Vice Head, Head of Organization, Head of Screening, Head of Pharmacy.
- **Courses (الكورسات)** — free for medical students: BLS / cardiac emergencies, suturing & wound care, injections & IV cannulation, ICC course, online HTN lecture series (with بنيان).
- **Events (الإيفنتات)** — «ما وراء الطب | Beyond Medicine» conference, 4 Sept 2026, Resala School – Mokattam, 7 speakers (packed auditorium). «Gray's Moments – جمعة الخير» visit to Resala Hospital Faisal. Ramadan food-bag packing & distribution in شبين القناطر. Annual "third" celebration (23 Jan 2026). Family Day. Iftar meals for «كأس رسالة».
- **Leadership** — heads & supervisors (clinic heads, event role cards).
- **Honors** — «هيرو الخير» plaques, best volunteer of the convoy/month, best heads of the quarter.
- **Awareness** — infographics: diabetes, flu, smoking, antibiotics, digital eye strain, heatstroke, burns first aid, painkillers, botulism (فسيخ), kidneys, breast cancer, breastfeeding.
- Links: facebook.com/atebaaelkheirnasrcity · resala.org/atba2-alkhyr

## Information architecture
One **landing page** that summarizes everything, plus a **tab / sub-landing per section** that goes deeper (each tab feels like its own mini landing page):
1. Hero (who we are + primary CTAs: اتطوع معانا / اتبرع)
2. About / mission (short, human)
3. What we do — overview of the 4 pillars: القوافل · العيادات · الكورسات · التوعية (each links to its tab)
4. Convoys highlight (latest convoys, photos)
5. Events highlight (Beyond Medicine as flagship)
6. Courses highlight (hands-on training, register)
7. Our people — leadership + honors (volunteer recognition)
8. Gallery teaser (curated real photos)
9. Volunteer / donate CTA band
10. Footer (contact, Facebook, Resala)
Section tabs: /convoys · /clinics · /courses · /events · /team · /awareness · /gallery

## Language & tone
- Arabic, RTL. **Headlines in simple formal Arabic (فصحى بسيطة)**; small supporting lines, buttons and micro-copy may carry a light Egyptian colloquial touch.
- Warm, confident, respectful. Never pity-driven. Human and hopeful.
- Examples: H1 «رعاية طبية كريمة، تصل إلى من يحتاجها» · button «اتطوع معانا» · «كل جمعة في مكان جديد».

## Visual identity (inspired by Resala's system, but its own)
- Resala brand DNA (resala.org): primary navy **#27318B**, accent red **#E31B24**, light ground **#F5F8FF**, font **Cairo**. Atebaa El-Kheir logo = navy shield with three figures and stethoscopes.
- Our own spin: keep navy as the anchor and use red sparingly (a pulse, a mark, a key CTA). Allow one supporting tint derived from navy (soft periwinkle / mist blue) and warm off-white paper tones for breathing room.
- Typography: a distinctive Arabic display face (not generic) paired with a highly legible Arabic body face; Latin names in a matching grotesk.
- Feel: **simple + formal + creative + modern**, "calm luxury" — refined, generous whitespace, nothing loud or generic. Creative imagery, not just bold blocks.
- Must NOT look AI-generated or templated: no purple gradients, no emoji icons, no identical rounded cards everywhere, no stock "hero with centered text" cliché.

## Imagery
- Use real photos from the team (uploaded assets): convoy exams, clinic BP/sugar checks, eye exam with slit lamp, CPR training, suturing practice, Beyond Medicine auditorium & stage, team at Resala Hospital, Ramadan distribution at dusk.
- Treat photos with care: consistent color grading, creative crops/masks (e.g. shield-shaped or pill-shaped masks echoing the logo), overlapping compositions, subtle duotone where it helps unify.
- Custom illustrations/line-art welcome: medical line icons, stethoscope/ECG-inspired line drawings, map/route lines for convoys, hand-drawn accents.

## Motion (key differentiator)
- Calm, premium, purposeful animations — nothing gimmicky:
  - scroll-driven reveals (masks opening on photos, text lines rising)
  - a drawn line (stethoscope tube / pulse / convoy route) that draws itself as you scroll and connects sections
  - subtle parallax on photo layers, magnetic/soft hover on CTAs, marquee of convoy names, counters only for real numbers
  - respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Mobile-first: motion must be smooth on phones; thumb-friendly 44px+ targets; sticky bottom CTA bar on mobile.

## Mobile
Mobile is the primary target (most visitors come from Facebook on phones). Design at 390px first, then scale up to desktop.
